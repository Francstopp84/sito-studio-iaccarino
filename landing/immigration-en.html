FAQ

How much does a residence permit renewal cost?

Fees are agreed in writing before the mandate. A standard renewal starts at €250-400 + VAT. Appeals against denials vary with complexity.

Can I appeal a denial by the Questura?

Yes. You can appeal to TAR Campania within 60 days of notification. Interim suspension is often available.

Do you assist in English?

Yes. Full English support over phone, WhatsApp, email, and in meetings at our Naples office.

How long does Italian citizenship by residence take?

Statutory term is 24 months, extendable to 36. If exceeded, we can file a TAR Lazio appeal to compel a decision.

Do you help clients held at CPR (repatriation centres)?

Yes. This is urgent — call +39 333 568 4659. We file opposition to the Justice of the Peace or appeal to TAR depending on the measure.
