Arrest: What to do in the first 48 hours

7 marzo 2026 — Avv. Francesco Iaccarino — Criminal Law — Reading time: 6 min

Receiving news that a family member has been arrested is a moment of panic. The first 48 hours are decisive: during this timeframe, the validation hearing (udienza di convalida) takes place before the Judge for Preliminary Investigations (GIP), which will determine whether the person remains in prison, is placed under house arrest (arresti domiciliari), or is released. Knowing what to do — and above all what not to do — can change the outcome.

As a criminal defense lawyer in Naples, I deal on a daily basis with validation hearings (udienze di convalida) at the Court of Naples (Tribunale di Napoli) and its detached sections. In this guide, I explain step by step the rights of the arrested person and the most effective defense strategies.

The rights of the arrested person: what the law provides

Art. 386 c.p.p. — Duties of the judicial police (polizia giudiziaria)

The judicial police (polizia giudiziaria) must give immediate notice of the arrest to the Public Prosecutor (PM) and to defense counsel. The arrested person has the right to communicate with family members and to appoint a defense lawyer of choice (avvocato di fiducia).

The law guarantees the arrested person a series of fundamental rights that no one can deny:

  • Right to legal counsel — from the very first moment, you can appoint a defense lawyer of choice (avvocato di fiducia). If you do not have one, a court-appointed lawyer (difensore d'ufficio) is assigned.
  • Right to silence — you are under no obligation to answer questions. Silence cannot be used against you.
  • Right to communicate with family members — the police must allow you to inform at least one family member or cohabitant of the arrest.
  • Right to know the charges — you must be informed clearly of the offenses alleged against you.
  • Right to an interpreter — if you do not speak Italian, you have the right to a free interpreter for the entire duration of the proceedings.

Practical advice. Never make statements to the police without your lawyer present. Even seemingly harmless statements can be used against you. Exercise your right to silence until defense counsel has examined the case files.

Timeline of the first 48 hours

An arrest triggers a sequence of events with strict deadlines established by the code of criminal procedure (codice di procedura penale). Here is what happens, hour by hour.

Moment of arrest

The judicial police (polizia giudiziaria) proceed with the arrest in flagrante delicto (arresto in flagranza, art. 380-381 c.p.p.). The arrest record (verbale di arresto) is drawn up setting forth the grounds and circumstances. The arrested person is taken to a police station or barracks (caserma or commissariato).

Notification to the Public Prosecutor (PM) and defense counsel

The police immediately notify the Public Prosecutor (Pubblico Ministero - PM) on duty and the appointed defense counsel (or the court-appointed lawyer). The Public Prosecutor (PM) may proceed with questioning the arrested person, always in the presence of defense counsel.

Application for validation by the Public Prosecutor (PM)

Within 24 hours, the Public Prosecutor (PM) must ask the Judge for Preliminary Investigations (GIP) to validate the arrest. If this is not done, the arrested person must be released immediately. This is a mandatory deadline: failure to comply renders the arrest unlawful.

Validation hearing before the Judge for Preliminary Investigations (GIP)

Within 48 hours of the arrest, the Judge for Preliminary Investigations (GIP) holds the validation hearing (udienza di convalida). Everything is decided here: release, house arrest (arresti domiciliari), prison, or other precautionary measures (misure cautelari).

The validation hearing: the decisive moment

The validation hearing (udienza di convalida) is the most important moment of the first 48 hours. The Judge for Preliminary Investigations (GIP) must evaluate two distinct issues:

  1. The legality of the arrest — was the arrest carried out in compliance with the law? Was there truly an in flagrante delicto situation (flagranza di reato)?
  2. The application of a precautionary measure (misura cautelare) — do precautionary requirements (esigenze cautelari: risk of flight, tampering with evidence, reiteration of the offense) exist that justify a restriction of liberty?
Art. 274 c.p.p. — Precautionary requirements (esigenze cautelari)

Precautionary measures (misure cautelari) can be ordered only when specific requirements exist: risk of tampering with evidence, flight risk, or risk of reoffending. Without at least one of these requirements, the judge cannot apply any measure.

Possible decisions of the Judge for Preliminary Investigations (GIP)

At the end of the hearing, the judge may rule in several ways:

  • Non-validation + immediate release — the arrest was unlawful. The person is released immediately.
  • Validation without precautionary measure — the arrest was lawful, but there are no precautionary requirements (esigenze cautelari). Immediate release.
  • Validation + non-custodial measure — duty to report to the judicial police (obbligo di presentazione alla PG), order of compulsory residence (obbligo di dimora), restraining order (divieto di avvicinamento).
  • Validation + house arrest (arresti domiciliari) — the person remains at home under restrictions.
  • Validation + pre-trial detention in prison (custodia cautelare in carcere) — the most severe measure, applied only for serious offenses and in the presence of concrete precautionary requirements.

What the family member must do: practical guide

If a loved one has been arrested in Naples or in Campania, here are the immediate steps to take:

  1. Contact a criminal defense lawyer immediately — a lawyer who deals with criminal law on a daily basis. The validation hearing (udienza di convalida) takes place within 48 hours, and preparation is crucial.
  2. Gather information — where are they being held? At which barracks, police station (commissariato), or remand prison (casa circondariale)? What are the alleged offenses?
  3. Bring useful documents — identity card, tax code (codice fiscale), any medical certificates, proof of residence. These are needed to request house arrest (arresti domiciliari).
  4. Do not speak to the press — any public statement may be used in the proceedings.
  5. Prepare a letter of reference — an employer, parish priest, or other figures can attest to good conduct. These assist the judge in the assessment.

Defense strategies at the validation hearing

At a validation hearing (udienza di convalida), a criminal defense lawyer evaluates various strategies:

Challenging the in flagrante status

Arrest in flagrante delicto (arresto in flagranza) presupposes that the person was caught in the act of committing the offense or immediately thereafter. If the police intervened too late or on the basis of vague reports, the flagrante condition can be challenged and the arrest declared unlawful.

Ruling out precautionary requirements

Even if the arrest is validated, the judge must verify whether current and concrete precautionary requirements (esigenze cautelari) exist. A person with no criminal record, stable employment, family, and a fixed residence is unlikely to present a risk of flight or reoffending.

Requesting a less restrictive measure

The principle of proportionality (art. 275 c.p.p.) requires the judge to apply the least burdensome measure among those suitable. If house arrest (arresti domiciliari) is sufficient to protect the precautionary requirements, detention in prison cannot be ordered.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a lawyer cost for a validation hearing?

The fee varies according to the complexity of the case. Our firm offers an initial consultation at 200 euros, deductible from any subsequent defense mandate. In case of arrest, it is essential not to compromise on defense: the first 48 hours determine the outcome of the entire proceedings.

Can I visit my family member in prison before the hearing?

Prior to the validation hearing (udienza di convalida), visits with family members may be authorized by the Public Prosecutor (PM). Defense counsel, on the other hand, always has the right to access and confer with their client.

What happens after the validation hearing?

If a precautionary measure is applied, defense counsel may immediately appeal it before the Review Court (Tribunale del Riesame, art. 309 c.p.p.) within 10 days. The Review Court re-evaluates the entire matter and may revoke or substitute the measure.
