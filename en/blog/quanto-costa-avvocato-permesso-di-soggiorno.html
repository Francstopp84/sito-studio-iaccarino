How much does a lawyer cost for a residence permit (permesso di soggiorno)

November 2, 2026 — Avv. Francesco Iaccarino — Immigration — Reading time: 5 min

This is the first question almost everyone asks, and rightly so. However, there is no single fixed price: the cost depends on what is genuinely required. An administrative procedure at the Police Headquarters (Questura) is one matter; an appeal (ricorso) before the Court against a refusal is another. Here is how to assess the matter transparently.

What determines the cost

The lawyer's fee does not depend on the «type of permit» in the abstract, but on the activity required. In general, three situations can be distinguished:

  • Administrative procedure: renewal, conversion (for example from study to work), residence card (carta di soggiorno), family reunification. This involves preparing and reviewing the application and documents and monitoring the procedure.
  • Refusal or revocation: the Police Headquarters (Questura) or the Prefecture (Prefettura) rejects the application. In this case, it is necessary to assess the decision and, where the legal grounds exist, lodge an appeal (ricorso) within the deadlines, which are short.
  • Proceedings before the court: international and special protection, expulsion, detention. These are judicial proceedings, involving hearings and written pleadings.

In addition to the lawyer's fee, there are the out-of-pocket expenses for the matter (payment slips, duty stamps, court filing fee (contributo unificato) where due), which vary depending on the procedure and are specified beforehand.

When the State pays

In certain proceedings, individuals with an income below the statutory threshold may apply for admission to legal aid (patrocinio a spese dello Stato): in that case, the lawyer's fee is not paid by the client. The relevant requirements are assessed during the initial consultation.

Pay attention to deadlines

The highest cost is often waiting. Against a refusal or an expulsion, the deadlines to act are short and, once they have expired, the available options are greatly reduced. If you have received a formal decision, do not delay.
