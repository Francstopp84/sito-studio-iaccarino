Debts and cars: when is it possible to avoid foreclosure (pignoramento)?

November 14, 2026 — Avv. Francesco Iaccarino — Tax Law

The concrete problem: the risk of losing your car during liquidation

Many citizens who find themselves in a situation of severe financial crisis rightly fear the consequences of controlled liquidation (liquidazione controllata). When the burden of debt becomes unsustainable and one accesses the procedures provided for by the Business Crisis Code (Codice della crisi d'impresa), the main fear is losing every asset, including the car. For those living in areas where public transport is lacking, or for those who must reach their workplace during night hours or in isolated industrial zones, the car is not a luxury item, but a tool for survival.

Currently, the regulations provide that the liquidator (liquidatore) takes charge of all the debtor's assets to sell them and satisfy the creditors. Often, even a small car of little economic value is taken from the owner, causing enormous damage to their ability to generate income or assist vulnerable family members. This situation creates a paradox: depriving the debtor of the only means that allows them to work means, in effect, preventing them from honoring the repayment plan or supporting their family, making the debt discharge (esdebitazione) procedure much more complex and painful.

What the regulations provide and the upcoming news

The Business Crisis Code (d.lgs. n. 14/2019) regulates the procedures for managing over-indebtedness (sovraindebitamento). Art. 268, co. 4, establishes which assets are excluded from liquidation, generally citing non-seizable credits or sums necessary for the debtor's maintenance. However, the car has never been explicitly protected. The novelty, introduced by an amendment to the bill for the conversion of decree-law n. 168/2026, aims to fill this gap by introducing specific protection for vehicles necessary for daily life.

It is essential to emphasize that this provision is currently under examination by the Senate. Once definitively approved, it will radically change the approach to liquidation. The rule introduces a value threshold, set at 3,000 euros, which acts as a watershed for the management of the asset. If the car's value falls within this figure, the waiver of the sale by the liquidator (liquidatore) becomes automatic, provided that the requirements of necessity are met. If the value is higher, the decision is not precluded, but is left to a case-by-case assessment, balancing the creditor's interest with that of the debtor.

The requirements to save the car: work, care, and study

Simply owning a car is not enough to request its exclusion from liquidation. The law requires strict conditions that the debtor must prove with extreme precision. First of all, it must be the only car owned by the subject. It is therefore not possible to save multiple vehicles or means other than cars, such as motorcycles or vans. The rationale of the rule is to protect the bare necessities for the dignity and work continuity of the family unit.

The permitted purposes are three and must be documented in an irrefutable manner:

  • Work: it is necessary to demonstrate that the car is indispensable for reaching the workplace, especially in the absence of public transport alternatives or due to particular shifts.
  • Care: the car must be used to accompany the debtor or a family member to medical visits, constant therapies, or rehabilitation centers.
  • Study: the vehicle must be necessary to attend school or university institutes, if there are no adequate connections.

What to do step by step: the practical procedure

If you find yourself in a situation of over-indebtedness (sovraindebitamento) in Naples or elsewhere, the first step is not to act on impulse. The request for the exclusion of the car is not automatic: it is up to the debtor to take action. It is necessary to prepare a documentary file that proves the necessity of the vehicle. If the car is worth less than 3,000 euros, the procedure is simplified, but proof of need remains an essential prerequisite to avoid disputes.

Here is how to proceed:

  1. Valuation of value: verify the real market quotation of the vehicle, not the purchase price.
  2. Gathering evidence: for work, obtain the contract and hours; for health, medical certificates and the therapeutic plan; for study, enrollment and the distance from home.
  3. Formal request: submit the request to the liquidator (liquidatore) appointed by the court, clearly explaining why the car is indispensable.
  4. Monitoring: wait for the outcome of the assessment, keeping in mind that for vehicles worth more than 3,000 euros, the liquidator (liquidatore) will perform a balancing act between your need and the creditors' right.

Errors to avoid and technical terms

A common mistake is to confuse the purchase value with the market value. The liquidator (liquidatore) will refer to the current quotation of the vehicle, which is often much lower than what was paid years earlier. Another mistake is to omit the necessary documentation: presenting a generic request without attaching medical certificates or employment contracts will almost certainly lead to the rejection of the application. Remember that transparency is the pillar of any over-indebtedness (sovraindebitamento) procedure.

Also pay attention to the terms: controlled liquidation (liquidazione controllata) is a complex insolvency procedure. Do not attempt to hide assets or transfer ownership of the car to third parties shortly before the opening of the procedure. Such acts could be revoked and compromise the debt discharge (esdebitazione), i.e., the possibility of canceling residual debts. Relying on professionals who know the Business Crisis Code (Codice della crisi d'impresa) well is essential to avoid missteps that could cost you dearly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I keep the car if it is worth more than 3,000 euros?

Yes, it is possible, but it is not automatic. The liquidator (liquidatore) will evaluate on a case-by-case basis whether the car is actually indispensable for work, health, or study, balancing your need with the interest of the creditors.

What must I prove to save the car?

You must prove that the car is the only one you own and that it is necessary for work, care, or study needs, attaching documents such as contracts, medical certificates, or therapeutic plans.

Is the new rule already in force?

The amendment has been approved by the Chamber and is currently under examination by the Senate. It will become operational only after the final approval and the conversion into law of decree-law n. 168/2026.
