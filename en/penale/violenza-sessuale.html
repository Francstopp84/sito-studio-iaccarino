Accused of sexual assault?
Your defence must be impeccable.

Accusations of sexual assault are among the most serious in our legal system. With the introduction of the "Red Code" (Codice Rosso, L. 69/2019), procedural timeframes have been drastically accelerated and penalties increased. The person under investigation often faces immediate precautionary measures (misure cautelari) — pre-trial detention in prison (custodia in carcere), house arrest (arresti domiciliari), or restraining orders (divieto di avvicinamento) — even prior to trial. In this context, a timely, rigorous, and qualified technical defence is not an option: it is a necessity.

Relevant legal provision

What the law provides

Italian Criminal Code (Codice Penale)

Art. 609-bis — Sexual assault

Whoever, through violence or threat or by abuse of authority, compels someone to commit or undergo sexual acts shall be punished with imprisonment from six to twelve years. The same penalty shall apply to whoever induces someone to commit or undergo sexual acts by abusing the physical or psychological impairment of the offended person at the time of the act, or by misleading the offended person through impersonating another person.

Base penalty: imprisonment from 6 to 12 years
Italian Criminal Code (Codice Penale)

Art. 609-ter — Aggravating circumstances

The penalty is increased (from 7 to 14 years) if the acts are committed against a person under the age of fourteen, with the use of weapons or alcoholic/narcotic substances, by a disguised person or someone impersonating a public official, against a person subject to restrictions of personal liberty, or against a person under the age of sixteen of whom the perpetrator is the ascendant, parent (including adoptive parent), or legal guardian.

Aggravated penalty: imprisonment from 7 to 14 years
Italian Criminal Code (Codice Penale)

Art. 609-octies — Gang sexual assault

Gang sexual assault consists of the participation, by multiple persons acting together, in acts of sexual assault pursuant to art. 609-bis. The penalty is imprisonment from twelve to twenty-four years. Anyone participating in the act is punished, even without materially performing the violence.

Gang offence penalty: imprisonment from 12 to 24 years

Offences provided for by the code

Art. 609-bis — Basic offence of sexual assault

Imprisonment from 6 to 12 years. Compulsion through violence, threat, or abuse of authority to commit or undergo sexual acts. It also includes inducement by abusing conditions of physical or mental impairment. In cases of lesser gravity, the penalty is reduced by up to two-thirds.

Art. 609-ter — Specific aggravating circumstances

Imprisonment from 7 to 14 years. Violence against minors under 14 years of age, use of weapons or substances, disguise, abuse of family relationships or custody. If the victim is under 10 years of age, the penalty is imprisonment from 12 to 24 years.

Art. 609-octies — Gang sexual assault

Imprisonment from 12 to 24 years. Participation of multiple persons assembled together in acts of sexual assault. Liability extends to all participants, including those who did not physically execute the act. The mitigating circumstance of lesser gravity is not applicable.

Special legislation

Art. 609-quater — Sexual acts with a minor

Whoever engages in sexual acts with a person under the age of fourteen (or sixteen, if the perpetrator is an ascendant, parent, guardian, or other person to whom the minor is entrusted), outside the circumstances of violence or threat, shall be subject to the penalty provided for under art. 609-bis. The act is punishable irrespective of the minor's consent.

Penalty: imprisonment from 6 to 12 years (same penalty as art. 609-bis)

"Red Code" (Codice Rosso, L. 69/2019): The law has imposed on the Public Prosecutor (Pubblico Ministero) the obligation to interview the injured party within 3 days of the registration of the report of an offence (notizia di reato), drastically accelerating the investigative process. It has introduced new criminal offences (disfigurement of appearance through permanent facial injuries, unlawful dissemination of sexually explicit images) and increased penalties for sexual offences. Arrest in flagrante delicto (arresto in flagranza) is mandatory. Precautionary measures (misure cautelari) are ordered with greater frequency and strictness. For the person under investigation, this means that the time available to organize a defence is extremely reduced.

How we defend you

Defence strategies

Allegations of sexual assault require a technical defence of the highest standard. Our law firm examines every element in the case file — statements, scientific evidence, wiretaps, phone records — to construct a rigorous defence strategy, respecting the dignity of all parties involved while effectively protecting the rights of the accused.

Consensual nature of the relationship

In-depth analysis of the relational context between the parties. Reconstruction of the dynamics of the relationship before and after the alleged incident through chats, text messages, and witness testimonies. Demonstrating the existence of a consensual relationship, where supported by the evidence.

Absence of violence or threat

Challenging the constituent element of the offence: the absence of violence, threat, or abuse of authority. If there was neither compulsion nor inducement through abuse of impairment, the characteristic criminal conduct is absent. Verification of the absence of physical signs, negative medical reports, and alternative factual reconstruction.

Credibility of the offended person

The statement of the offended person can, on its own, constitute proof of guilt, but it must be subjected to a particularly rigorous credibility assessment. Analysis of any contradictions, inconsistencies, alterations in the account, and collateral motives (family disputes, separations, financial contentions).

Psychological expert opinion and technical consultancy

Appointment of expert witnesses for the defence (consulenti tecnici di parte — forensic psychologists) to assess the reliability of the injured party’s account, the suggestibility of the declarant (especially if a minor), and the compliance of interview procedures with the guidelines of the Carta di Noto and international protocols.

Flaws in wiretaps and eavesdropping

Verification of the legality of the authorization decrees for telephone and environmental wiretaps. Challenging the admissibility of recordings ordered in violation of art. 267 c.p.p. Analysis of the accurate transcription and interpretation of intercepted conversations, which are often decisive in these proceedings.

Analysis of telephone records and chats

Forensic examination of phone records, WhatsApp messages, text messages, social media, and digital communications between the parties. Reconstruction of the timeline of events, verification of the spatio-temporal consistency of accounts, and identification of exculpatory elements often overlooked by the investigation.

Challenging precautionary measures

Immediate appeal against pre-trial detention orders in prison or under house arrest. Application for review (riesame) before the Review Court (Tribunale della Libertà, art. 309 c.p.p.) and, where necessary, appeal to the Court of Cassation (ricorso per cassazione). Demonstration of the absence of serious indications of guilt (gravi indizi di colpevolezza) or of precautionary requirements (esigenze cautelari).

Results achieved

5 cases resolved with a positive outcome

Case no. 1

Consensual relationship and witness statement contradictions — Tribunale di Napoli

Charge: art. 609-bis c.p. — Risk: imprisonment from 6 to 12 years + precautionary measure (misura cautelare)

A 38-year-old man was accused of sexual assault (violenza sessuale) by his former partner, in the context of a highly conflictual separation involving a child custody dispute. The complaint had been filed four months after the alleged events. The defense obtained and analyzed over 1.200 WhatsApp messages exchanged between the parties in the period following the alleged incident, which unequivocally demonstrated the continuation of an affectionate and consensual intimate relationship.

The analysis of the statements made by the injured party (persona offesa) during the complaint, the pre-trial evidentiary hearing (incidente probatorio), and the trial hearing (dibattimento) revealed significant contradictions regarding the dynamics of the events, the times, and the presence of third parties. Following the trial, the Court fully acquitted the defendant because the fact does not exist (perché il fatto non sussiste).

Full acquittal — The offense was not committed (il fatto non sussiste)
Case no. 2

Absence of coercive conduct — Tribunale di Roma

Charge: art. 609-bis c.p. — Risk: imprisonment up to 12 years + pre-trial detention in prison (custodia cautelare in carcere)

A 45-year-old professional was arrested under an order for pre-trial detention in prison (ordinanza di custodia cautelare in carcere) for sexual assault against a colleague. The defense immediately appealed the precautionary measure before the Review Court (Tribunale del Riesame), obtaining the replacement of detention in prison with house arrest (arresti domiciliari), highlighting the absence of any danger of flight or re-offending.

During the trial phase, the defense proved, through surveillance camera footage from the workplace and testimony from colleagues present, that the encounter between the parties had occurred in a context devoid of any coercive element. The absence of medical reports certifying signs of physical violence and the alternative reconstruction of events led the Court to an acquittal because the act does not constitute an offense (perché il fatto non costituisce reato), due to the absence of the element of violence or threat.

Acquittal — The act does not constitute an offense (il fatto non costituisce reato)
Case no. 3

Procedural flaws in the protected hearing of the minor (audizione protetta) — Tribunale di Napoli Nord

Charge: art. 609-quater c.p. — Risk: imprisonment from 6 to 12 years + entry in the sex offender registry (registro dei reati sessuali)

A teacher was accused of sexual acts with an underage student based solely on the girl’s statements, given during a pre-trial evidentiary hearing (incidente probatorio). The defense appointed a party-appointed technical expert (consulente tecnico di parte) — a forensic psychologist experienced in interviewing minors — who highlighted serious violations of the Carta di Noto protocols during the protected hearing (audizione protetta): leading questions, failure to respect the free recall phase, and lack of adequate documentation of the methodology used.

The expert also highlighted the possible influence of the family environment on the minor’s account, in a context of intense conflict between the girl’s family and the school. The Court, upholding the defense arguments, deemed the statements insufficiently reliable and acquitted the defendant due to the groundlessness of the fact (per insussistenza del fatto).

Acquittal — Statements deemed unreliable
Case no. 4

Reclassification and mitigating circumstance of minor gravity (minore gravità) — Corte d’Appello di Napoli

Charge: art. 609-bis c.p. — First-instance sentence: 6 years of imprisonment

A 32-year-old man was convicted at first instance to 6 years of imprisonment for sexual assault (violenza sessuale). The defense, taking on the mandate at the appeal stage, fully re-examined the case file and identified decisive elements not adequately weighed by the first judge: the brevity and nature of the alleged conduct, limited to a single incident of sudden touching, and the absence of physical violence or explicit threats.

The Court of Appeal (Corte d’Appello) granted the request for application of the mitigating circumstance of minor gravity pursuant to art. 609-bis, paragraph 3, c.p., redetermining the penalty at 2 years of imprisonment with a suspended sentence (sospensione condizionale). The defendant avoided serving time in prison.

Sentence reduced from 6 to 2 years — Suspended sentence (sospensione condizionale) granted
Case no. 5

Inadmissible wiretaps and exculpatory telephone records — Tribunale di Salerno

Charge: art. 609-bis c.p. with aggravating circumstance under art. 609-ter — Risk: imprisonment from 7 to 14 years

A 50-year-old man was accused of aggravated sexual assault against a woman in conditions of psychological inferiority. The prosecution's case was largely based on telephone and ambient wiretappings. The defense raised the inadmissibility of the wiretaps (inutilizzabilità), proving that the authorizing decree issued by the preliminary investigations judge (GIP) lacked adequate reasoning regarding the grave indications of a crime and the absolute indispensability for the continuation of the investigation, in violation of art. 267 c.p.p.

At the same time, the forensic analysis of telephone records demonstrated that the defendant was in a different location from the one indicated by the injured party at the time of one of the three alleged incidents, providing an irrefutable alibi. The Court declared the wiretappings inadmissible and, with the core of the prosecution collapsing, acquitted the defendant on all counts with the fullest formula (formula piena).

Full acquittal — Wiretaps excluded, alibi confirmed

Are you under investigation or on trial?

Do not wait. In these cases, every hour counts.

Accusations of sexual assault entail immediate and devastating consequences: precautionary measures (misure cautelari), restrictions on personal liberty, and irreparable reputational damage. Under the "Red Code" (Codice Rosso), deadlines are extremely tight. A timely and qualified defence intervention can make the difference between detention in prison and freedom, between an unjust conviction and an acquittal.
