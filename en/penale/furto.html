Relevant legal provision

What the law provides

Italian Criminal Code (Codice Penale)

Art. 624 — Theft

Whoever takes possession of movable property belonging to another, taking it away from whoever holds it, for the purpose of obtaining a profit for oneself or for others, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to three years and with a fine from 154 euros to 516 euros. Theft is punishable upon complaint by the victim (querela della persona offesa), unless the aggravating circumstances set forth in art. 625 c.p. apply.

Penalty: imprisonment from 6 months to 3 years + fine from € 154 to € 516
Italian Criminal Code (Codice Penale)

Art. 624-bis — Residential burglary and snatch theft (furto in abitazione e furto con strappo)

Whoever takes possession of movable property belonging to another, depriving the holder of it, by entering a building or other place intended wholly or partly as a private dwelling, or its appurtenances, shall be punished with imprisonment (reclusione) from four to seven years and with a fine (multa) from EUR 927 to EUR 1,500. The same penalty applies to whoever takes possession of movable property belonging to another by snatching it from the hand or person of the offended party. The offence is prosecuted ex officio (procedibile d’ufficio).

Penalty: imprisonment from 4 to 7 years + fine from € 927 to € 1,500

The different types of theft

Simple theft (art. 624 c.p.)

Prosecuted upon complaint (procedibile a querela). Imprisonment from 6 months to 3 years + fine from € 154 to € 516. Possibility of withdrawal of the complaint (remissione della querela) and exclusion of punishability due to the particular minor gravity of the act (particolare tenuità del fatto, art. 131-bis c.p.).

Aggravated theft (art. 625 c.p.)

Prosecuted ex officio (procedibile d’ufficio). Imprisonment from 1 to 6 years + fine from € 309 to € 1,032. Aggravating circumstances: damage to property, dexterity, fraudulent means, exposure to public trust, three or more persons acting together, act committed on property in public offices or facilities.

Theft in a dwelling (art. 624-bis c.p.)

Prosecuted ex officio (procedibile d’ufficio). Autonomous and serious offence. Imprisonment from 4 to 7 years + fine from € 927 to € 1,500. Includes entry into a building or place intended as a private dwelling. Precautionary measures are frequent. Conditional suspension of sentence is excluded above 2 years.

Theft by dexterity (art. 625 n. 4 c.p.)

Prosecuted ex officio (procedibile d’ufficio). Imprisonment from 1 to 6 years. Applies when the act is committed with particular skill in taking advantage of a momentary distraction of the victim (pickpocketing, bag snatching without violence). Frequently charged in urban centers and on public transit.

Please note — Cartabia Reform and particular minor gravity of the act: With the reform under D.Lgs. 150/2022, art. 131-bis c.p. has broadened the scope of application of the ground for non-punishability due to the particular minor gravity of the act (particolare tenuità del fatto), extending it to offences punishable by a custodial sentence not exceeding two years at the minimum. Simple theft (art. 624 c.p.) falls fully within this scope.

How we defend you

Defence strategies

Behind every accusation of theft lie specific circumstances that can completely overturn the outcome of the trial. Our firm examines every detail of the case file to build the most effective defence.

Minor gravity of the act (art. 131-bis c.p.)

When the value of the property taken is modest and the conduct is not habitual, it is possible to obtain a declaration of non-punishability due to the particular minor gravity of the act (particolare tenuità del fatto).

Lack of specific intent (dolo specifico)

Theft requires specific intent (dolo specifico) aimed at «making a profit». If the taking was not intended to obtain an economic advantage — for instance due to mistake, distraction, or the belief of having a lawful right to the property — the mental element (mens rea) of the offence is missing.

Theft for temporary use (art. 626 c.p.)

If the property taken is returned immediately after momentary use, the offence of theft for temporary use (furto d’uso) is established, which carries a significantly reduced penalty (imprisonment up to 1 year or a fine up to € 206) and is prosecuted upon complaint (procedibile a querela). Reclassifying the act can radically change the procedural outcome.

State of necessity (art. 54 c.p.)

A person who acted to avoid an actual danger of serious harm to their person, which was otherwise unavoidable, is not punishable. Theft driven by hunger or to provide for vital personal or family necessities may qualify for the defence of state of necessity (stato di necessità), resulting in full acquittal.

Procedural and evidentiary flaws

Challenging the lawfulness of identification procedures, the methods of acquiring video recordings, and the chain of custody of evidence. The inadmissibility of unlawfully obtained evidence (art. 191 c.p.p.) can lead to an acquittal due to insufficient evidence.

Alternative procedures and probation (messa alla prova)

Evaluation of access to plea bargaining (patteggiamento, art. 444 c.p.p.), summary trial (giudizio abbreviato), or suspension of proceedings with probation (messa alla prova, art. 168-bis c.p.), offering the possibility of extinguishing the offence without any record appearing on the standard criminal record certificate (casellario giudiziale).

Compensation and restorative conduct

Full compensation for damages to the injured party (persona offesa), combined with the return of the stolen goods, may allow for the withdrawal of the criminal complaint (remissione della querela) (in cases of simple theft), access to alternative procedures with sentence reductions (riti premiali), or the application of the mitigating circumstance pursuant to art. 62 n. 6 c.p.

Results achieved

5 brilliantly resolved cases

Case no. 1

Supermarket theft — Minor nature of the act (tenuità del fatto) — Tribunale di Napoli

Charge: aggravated theft pursuant to art. 625 no. 2 c.p. — Risk: imprisonment up to 6 years

A 62-year-old woman with no criminal record stopped at a supermarket checkout with unpaid goods worth 27 euros concealed in her shopping bag. She was charged with aggravated theft on the grounds of goods exposed to public trust (esposizione alla pubblica fede). The defense demonstrated the client's completely clean criminal record, the negligible value of the goods, the absence of any prior record, and the non-habitual nature of the conduct.

The Court applied the ground for non-punishability due to the particular minor nature of the act (particolare tenuità del fatto) pursuant to art. 131-bis c.p., delivering a judgment of no case to answer (sentenza di non doversi procedere). No conviction, no penalty, no entry in the standard criminal record (casellario giudiziale).

Non-punishability due to minor nature of the act — No conviction
Case no. 2

Residential burglary — Inadmissible video evidence — Tribunale di Roma

Charge: art. 624-bis c.p. — Risk: imprisonment from 4 to 7 years + precautionary measure (misura cautelare)

A young factory worker accused of residential burglary on the basis of video recordings from a private surveillance system installed without the required privacy notice pursuant to art. 13 of the GDPR and without authorization from the Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). The man had been placed under the precautionary measure of house arrest (arresti domiciliari). The defense raised an objection regarding the inadmissibility (inutilizzabilità) of the video recordings pursuant to art. 191 c.p.p., as they were obtained in breach of statutory prohibitions.

The Court upheld the objection, declaring the video recordings inadmissible. Once the sole incriminating evidence was excluded, the defendant was acquitted for not having committed the act (per non aver commesso il fatto). The precautionary measure was immediately revoked.

Full acquittal — Precautionary measure revoked
Case no. 3

Unauthorized temporary use of a vehicle (furto d’uso) — Reclassification — Tribunale di Napoli Nord

Charge: aggravated theft pursuant to art. 624-625 c.p. — Risk: imprisonment up to 6 years

A university student accused of aggravated theft for having taken an acquaintance's car parked with the keys inside, using it for about two hours and returning it to the same spot. The defense produced evidence of immediate return following temporary use — witness testimonies and vehicle GPS data — requesting the reclassification of the offense as unauthorized temporary use (furto d’uso) pursuant to art. 626, paragraph 1, c.p.

The Court granted the request, reclassifying the offense from aggravated theft (sentence of up to 6 years) to unauthorized temporary use (furto d’uso, penalty of up to 1 year or a fine). The young man was sentenced solely to a fine of 200 euros, with the benefit of a suspended sentence (sospensione condizionale della pena) and non-disclosure on criminal records (non menzione).

Reclassification as unauthorized temporary use (furto d’uso) — Fine of € 200 only
Case no. 4

State of necessity (stato di necessità) — Food theft — Tribunale di Milano

Charge: aggravated theft pursuant to art. 625 c.p. — Risk: imprisonment up to 6 years

A 45-year-old unemployed and homeless man accused of aggravated theft for stealing foodstuffs worth 15 euros from a supermarket. The defense documented the client's condition of absolute indigence — social services certification, lack of income, lack of housing — and invoked the legal defense of state of necessity (stato di necessità) pursuant to art. 54 c.p., demonstrating that the man had acted to satisfy a vital and urgent need for food.

Acquittal due to state of necessity — The act does not constitute an offense
Case no. 5

Probation (messa alla prova) with extinguishment of the offense — Tribunale di Salerno

Charge: theft by dexterity (furto con destrezza) pursuant to art. 625 no. 4 c.p. — Risk: imprisonment from 1 to 6 years

A 24-year-old man with no criminal record accused of theft by dexterity (furto con destrezza) for stealing a mobile phone from a passenger's pocket on a bus. The theft had been recorded by on-board cameras, making a challenge on evidentiary grounds impossible. The defense strategy focused on immediate compensation for damages to the injured party and a request for suspension of the proceedings with probation (messa alla prova) pursuant to art. 168-bis c.p.

The Court granted probation (messa alla prova) with a program of 200 hours of community service (lavori di pubblica utilità) at a charitable organization. At the end of the period, the offense was declared extinguished with its deletion from the criminal record (casellario giudiziale). The young man was able to continue his career path without any criminal record.

Offense extinguished — No criminal record
