Accused of stalking?
Your personal liberty and your future are at risk.

The offence of harassment (atti persecutori - stalking) is among the most frequently prosecuted and feared in our legal system. With the Red Code (Codice Rosso - L. 69/2019) and subsequent amendments, penalties have been drastically increased and precautionary measures are imposed with extreme rapidity. An accusation of stalking can lead to a restraining order (divieto di avvicinamento), an electronic tag, or pre-trial detention in prison (custodia cautelare in carcere) — even prior to trial. However, not every complaint reflects the reality of the facts. A criminal defence lawyer can make the difference.

Relevant legal provision

What the law provides

Italian Criminal Code (Codice Penale)

Art. 612-bis — Harassment (Atti persecutori - Stalking)

Whoever, through reiterated conduct, threatens or harasses another person in such a manner as to cause a lasting and severe state of anxiety or fear, or to instil a well-founded fear for their own personal safety or that of a close relative or a person linked to them by an affective relationship, or to force them to alter their daily habits, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to six years and six months.

Base penalty: imprisonment from 1 year to 6 years and 6 months

Aggravating circumstances and the Red Code (Codice Rosso)

Specific aggravating circumstances

When the penalty is increased

The penalty is increased if the act is committed by a spouse, including if separated or divorced, or by a person who is or was linked to the victim by an affective relationship (former partner aggravating circumstance). The penalty is further increased by up to one-half if the act is committed against a minor, a pregnant woman, or a person with disabilities, or through digital or telecommunication tools (cyberstalking).

The Red Code (Codice Rosso) (L. 69/2019) introduced a fast-track procedure for investigations: the Public Prosecutor (PM) must hear the injured person within 3 days of the criminal complaint, and precautionary measures are ordered with extreme rapidity. Law no. 168/2023 further tightened the framework, providing for mandatory arrest in deferred flagrante delicto (arresto obbligatorio in flagranza differita) and expanding the use of electronic tags.

The severity categories

Basic offence

Imprisonment from 1 to 6 years and 6 months. Repeated conduct of threats or harassment without aggravating circumstances. Prosecutable upon a criminal complaint filed by the victim (querela) within a 6-month time limit. The withdrawal of the complaint (remissione della querela) can only take place in court.

Aggravated (former partner)

Penalty increased by up to one third. Act committed by a separated/divorced spouse or former cohabitant. Prosecutable ex officio (procedibile d’ufficio): withdrawal of the complaint is not possible. Frequent application of restraining orders (divieto di avvicinamento) and electronic tags.

Involving bodily injury or connected to serious crimes

Penalty increased by up to one-half. Act committed against minors, pregnant women, or persons with disabilities. Stalking connected to personal injury, sexual assault, or domestic abuse (maltrattamenti in famiglia). Ex officio prosecution (procedibilità d’ufficio). Tangible risk of pre-trial detention in prison (custodia cautelare in carcere).

Warning — Immediate precautionary measures: Unlike many other offences, for stalking, precautionary measures (restraining order prohibiting approach, removal from the family home, electronic bracelet, pre-trial detention in prison) are ordered extremely quickly, often even before the suspect is interviewed. It is essential to have a criminal defence lawyer from the very earliest stages in order to defend oneself effectively during validation hearings or review proceedings (riesame).

How we defend you

Defence strategies

Not every complaint for stalking corresponds to reality. Not every accusation withstands evidentiary scrutiny. Our law firm analyses every element of the case file to build the most effective defence, dismantling unfounded accusations and protecting the rights of the investigated person.

Absence of reiteration

The offence of stalking requires reiterated conduct: isolated episodes, even if unpleasant, do not constitute an offence under art. 612-bis c.p. We demonstrate that the alleged conduct is sporadic, circumscribed in time, and lacks the systematic nature required by the rule of law.

Absence of a state of anxiety in the victim

The provision requires a lasting and serious state of anxiety or fear, or an alteration of life habits. If the victim has not altered their habits, has not filed a complaint promptly, and presents no medical documentation of psychological distress, the consequence element of the offence is absent.

Reciprocity of conduct

In many high-conflict separations, harassing behaviours are reciprocal. If the alleged victim has themselves engaged in persecutory, provocative, or harassing conduct, the characterisation of unilateral stalking ceases to apply, paving the way for the exclusion of the offence or its reclassification.

Procedural flaws of the Red Code (Codice Rosso)

The Red Code (Codice Rosso) imposes strict deadlines on the Public Prosecutor (interview within 3 days). We verify compliance with deadlines, the correct recording of summary information, the lawfulness of precautionary measures, any omissions in discovery, and the actual existence of the serious circumstantial evidence required for restrictive measures.

Withdrawal of the complaint (Remissione della querela)

In cases prosecuted upon a formal complaint (querela) (simple cases, without aggravating circumstances), the formal withdrawal in court (remissione di querela) of the complaint extinguishes the offence. We assist the client in negotiations with the offended person, including through mediation and restorative justice pathways, to achieve an out-of-court settlement.

Alternative procedures and mitigating circumstances

Assessment of access to plea bargaining (patteggiamento, art. 444 c.p.p.), summary trial (giudizio abbreviato) with a one-third reduction of the penalty, probation with suspension of the trial (messa alla prova, art. 168-bis c.p.) including treatment and compensatory programs, as well as the assertion of general mitigating circumstances to obtain a proportionate sanction.

Results achieved

5 brilliantly resolved cases

Case no. 1

Instrumental criminal complaint in separation proceedings — Tribunale di Napoli

Charge: aggravated art. 612-bis c.p. (former spouse) — Risk: remand in custody + electronic monitoring bracelet

A forty-five-year-old entrepreneur reported by his wife during judicial separation proceedings for alleged stalking acts (atti persecutori) consisting of repeated telephone calls, messages, and waiting outside her home. A restraining order (divieto di avvicinamento) with an electronic monitoring bracelet had been ordered. The defense obtained telephone records and messaging history, demonstrating that communications exclusively concerned matters relating to the minor children and the administration of joint property.

On review, the Court of Liberty (Tribunale della Libertà) revoked the precautionary measure, finding that serious grounds of guilt were lacking. In the subsequent trial on the merits, the defendant was fully acquitted because the fact does not exist (perché il fatto non sussiste), after the instrumental nature of the complaint, aimed at obtaining advantages in the separation proceedings, came to light.

Full acquittal — Precautionary measure revoked
Case no. 2

Absence of change in living habits — Tribunale di Roma

Charge: art. 612-bis c.p. — Risk: imprisonment for up to 6 years and 6 months

A young professional reported by his former partner for stalking acts consisting of insistent messages and attempts at reconciliation following the end of the relationship. The defense proved that the alleged victim had in no way altered her living habits: she continued to frequent the same places, maintained her work and social routine unchanged, had never blocked the suspect’s number, and had not contacted anti-violence centers or mental health professionals.

The Court accepted the defense’s arguments, holding that, in the absence of an actual and documented change in the victim’s living habits, the harmful consequence of the offense provided for by art. 612-bis c.p. was lacking. The defendant was acquitted because the act does not constitute an offense (perché il fatto non costituisce reato).

Acquittal — Absence of the statutory consequence of the offense
Case no. 3

Reciprocal nature of conflictual conduct — Tribunale di Napoli Nord

Charge: aggravated art. 612-bis c.p. — Risk: imprisonment + restraining order (divieto di avvicinamento)

A thirty-eight-year-old craftsman reported by his former cohabitant for stalking following a cohabitation that ended in conflict. An examination of the case files revealed that both parties were responsible for mutual harassing behavior: the alleged victim had in turn sent hundreds of offensive messages, appeared repeatedly at the suspect’s workplace, and damaged his vehicle.

The defence produced in court chat logs, photographs, and witness evidence documenting the full reciprocity of the conduct. The judge ruled out one-way stalking, recognising a situation of mutual conflict and acquitting the defendant on the ground that the fact does not exist (perché il fatto non sussiste).

Acquittal — Mutual conflict established
Case no. 4

Procedural defects in the "Red Code" (Codice Rosso) procedure — Tribunale di Salerno

Charge: art. 612-bis c.p. with precautionary measure (misura cautelare) — Risk: pre-trial detention in prison (custodia cautelare in carcere)

A fifty-year-old shopkeeper placed under pre-trial detention in prison (custodia cautelare in carcere) for aggravated stalking against his ex-wife. The defence highlighted serious procedural violations: the Public Prosecutor (PM) had not heard the injured party within the three-day deadline prescribed by the Red Code (Codice Rosso), summary information had been gathered irregularly without procedural safeguards for the defence, and the precautionary measure had been ordered on the basis of a reconstruction of the facts based exclusively on the complainant's statements, without any objective corroboration.

Upon review (riesame), the Review Court (Tribunale della Libertà) annulled the precautionary order due to the lack of serious indications of guilt (gravi indizi di colpevolezza) and procedural defects. The person under investigation was immediately released. Subsequently, the proceedings concluded with dismissal (archiviazione).

Immediate release — Proceedings dismissed
Case no. 5

Probation (messa alla prova) with therapeutic programme — Tribunale di Napoli

Charge: art. 612-bis c.p. — Risk: imprisonment of up to 4 years + compromised criminal record

A thirty-two-year-old employee with no prior criminal record, reported by his ex-girlfriend for stalking conduct that continued for about two months after the end of their relationship. The factual findings were evidentially solid and showed no significant procedural defects. The defence strategy focused on applying for probation (messa alla prova) pursuant to art. 168-bis c.p., setting up a programme that included a therapeutic treatment course at a dedicated centre for addressing stalking behaviours, full compensation for damage to the injured party, and the performance of community service work (lavori di pubblica utilità).

The Court admitted probation (messa alla prova) with a 12-month programme. At the conclusion of the period, following confirmation of the positive outcome of the probation, the offence was declared extinguished with deletion from the criminal records registry (casellario giudiziale). The client was able to resume his life without a criminal record.

Offense extinguished — No criminal record

Do not delay. Every hour counts when your freedom is at stake.

Immediate intervention by defence counsel can prevent incarceration, secure the revocation of a restraining order, and build an effective defence strategy right from the start.
