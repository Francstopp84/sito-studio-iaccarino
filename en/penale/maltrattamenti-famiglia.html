Art. 572 Criminal Code — Red Code (Codice Rosso)

Accused of family mistreatment?
You risk imprisonment and removal from your children.

The offence of mistreatment in the family is among the most frequently prosecuted in the Italian criminal system. With the Red Code (Codice Rosso, Law 69/2019) and subsequent amendments under Law no. 168/2023, precautionary measures are immediate and penalties are very severe. However, not every family dispute constitutes mistreatment, and not every report corresponds to reality. A timely technical defence is essential.

Relevant legal provision

What the law provides

Italian Criminal Code (Codice Penale)

Art. 572 — Mistreatment against family members and cohabitants

Whoever mistreats a family member or cohabitant, or a person under their authority or entrusted to them for reasons of education, instruction, care, supervision, or custody, or for the exercise of a profession or art, shall be punished with imprisonment from three to seven years. The penalty is increased by up to one half if the act is committed in the presence of or to the detriment of a minor, a pregnant woman, or a person with a disability.

Penalty: imprisonment from 3 to 7 years (up to 12 with aggravating circumstances)

The degrees of severity

Basic circumstance (paragraph 1)

Mistreatment without aggravating circumstances. Imprisonment from 3 to 7 years. Covers reiterated conduct of physical or psychological oppression that habitually renders cohabitation intolerable.

Aggravated (paragraph 2)

In the presence of minors, pregnant women, or persons with disabilities. Penalty increased by up to one half. Even the occasional presence of a minor during the mistreating conduct constitutes the aggravating circumstance.

With grievous bodily harm or death (paragraphs 3-4)

Grievous bodily harm: 4-9 years. Very grievous bodily harm: 7-15 years. Death: 12-24 years. If the mistreatment results in grievous or very grievous personal injury, or the death of the victim, penalties are drastically increased.

Red Code (Codice Rosso, Law 69/2019) and Law 168/2023: Obligation for the Public Prosecutor (PM) to take statements from the victim within 3 days of the report. Possibility of immediate application of precautionary measures (removal from the family home, restraining order with electronic monitoring tag). Mandatory arrest in flagrante delicto, including delayed flagrancy (within 48 hours). Warning by the Police Chief (ammonimento del Questore). Immediate interim compensation (provvisionale) to the victim.

How we defend you

Defence strategies

The offence of mistreatment requires proof of the habitual nature of the conduct and its capacity to create an oppressive regime of life. Not every domestic quarrel constitutes the offence. The defence must dismantle the accusatory narrative through concrete evidence and contradictions.

Exclusion of habitual conduct

The offence requires the reiteration of conduct over time. Isolated episodes, even if violent, do not constitute art. 572 c.p. but may configure distinct offences (personal injury, threats) with significantly lower penalties. The defence analyses the timeline to rule out the requirement of habituality.

Mutual conflict

If both partners actively engage in the conflict, the position of subjugation of the victim that characterizes the offence is absent.

Instrumental complaint

In contentious separation contexts, a complaint for mistreatment may be used instrumentally to gain advantages regarding child custody or the assignment of the marital home. The defence reconstructs the context and timeline of the complaint to highlight its instrumental nature.

Review of precautionary measures

Immediate application for review (riesame) before the Court of Review (Tribunale della Libertà) to secure the revocation or replacement of the precautionary measure (removal order, restraining order) with less restrictive measures, demonstrating the lack of danger and the harm to the relationship with children.

Contradictions in the narrative

Analysis of the statements made by the injured party to identify contradictions, implausibilities, and incompatibilities with objective evidence (medical reports, text messages, third-party witness testimony). The credibility of the injured party is the core of the trial for mistreatment.

Rehabilitative pathway

Enrollment in a programme at a centre for perpetrators of violence (CAM) or a family mediation programme. While not extinguishing the offence, it demonstrates the defendant's awareness and can positively influence the sentencing and the granting of general mitigating circumstances.

Results achieved

5 brilliantly resolved cases

Case no. 1

Acquittal due to mutual conflict — Court of Naples

Charge: art. 572 c.p. — Risk: 3-7 years imprisonment + removal order

An entrepreneur was reported by his wife for domestic abuse (maltrattamenti in famiglia) during separation proceedings. The woman reported years of verbal abuse, humiliation, and financial control. The man was immediately removed from the family home under a restraining order barring proximity (divieto di avvicinamento).

The defense produced hundreds of WhatsApp messages demonstrating mutual and equal conflict, with insults and threats coming from both sides. Testimonies from family members and neighbors confirmed the absence of any position of subjugation on the part of the woman. The Court (Tribunale) acquitted the defendant on the merits because the alleged facts did not occur (perché il fatto non sussiste).

Full acquittal — Precautionary measures revoked
Case no. 2

Instrumental criminal complaint during separation proceedings — Tribunale di Roma

Charge: art. 572 c.p. + art. 612-bis c.p. (stalking) — Risk: imprisonment for 4-8 years

A father was reported by his former partner for domestic abuse (maltrattamenti) and stalking (atti persecutori) three days after filing a petition (ricorso) for joint custody of the children. The woman reported episodes of physical and psychological violence lasting for years, without ever having produced medical reports or filed prior complaints.

The defense reconstructed the timeline of the complaint, demonstrating the suspicious coincidence with the father's legal initiative. It submitted reports from social services describing a positive parenting relationship and the absence of indicators of violence. The statements of the injured party presented significant contradictions with objective evidence. The Court (Tribunale) acquitted the defendant of both charges.

Full acquittal — Joint custody confirmed
Case no. 3

Reclassification as bodily injury with suspended sentence — Tribunale di Salerno

Charge: art. 572 commi 1-2 c.p. — Risk: imprisonment for 4-10 years

A man was accused of domestic abuse (maltrattamenti) aggravated by the presence of minor children. The injured party produced two Emergency Department (Pronto Soccorso) medical reports relating to two distinct episodes six months apart.

The defense demonstrated that the two episodes, however serious, were isolated and did not fulfill the requirement of habitual conduct required by art. 572 c.p. It submitted statements from the minors' teachers, who had never reported situations of distress or abuse. The Court (Tribunale) reclassified the facts into two counts of intentional bodily injury (art. 582 c.p.), with a final sentence of 8 months of imprisonment, conditionally suspended (sospensione condizionale).

Reclassification as bodily injury — Suspended sentence
Case no. 4

Revocation of the electronic tag — Tribunale della Libertà di Napoli

Measure: restraining order barring proximity with electronic tag — Risk: pre-trial detention in prison (custodia cautelare in carcere)

A professional was subjected to a restraining order barring proximity to his ex-wife with the application of an electronic tag (braccialetto elettronico), following a complaint for domestic abuse (maltrattamenti). The man, who had no criminal record, lost the ability to see his children and suffered severe work-related repercussions.

The defense filed a motion for review (istanza di riesame) with the Review Court (Tribunale della Libertà), submitting documentation demonstrating the absence of dangerousness: clean criminal record, strong social and employment roots, and spontaneous enrollment in a program at a center for perpetrators of violence. The Court granted the motion, revoking the electronic tag and replacing the measure with a simple prohibition on communication, thereby allowing the resumption of contact with his children through social services.

Electronic tag revoked — Contact with children restored
Case no. 5

Acquittal on the merits — Corte d’Appello di Napoli

Charge: art. 572 c.p. — First-instance conviction: 3 years and 6 months

A woman was convicted at first instance of domestic abuse (maltrattamenti) against her elderly cohabiting mother. The prosecution had been triggered by a report from a social worker who had found precarious sanitary conditions in the home.

On appeal, the defense produced medical documentation concerning the daughter, who suffered from a diagnosed and certified severe form of depression, and testimonies from neighbors confirming the woman's dedication to her mother. It demonstrated that the sanitary conditions were the consequence of the daughter's illness and not of an intent to mistreat. The Court of Appeal (Corte d’Appello) overturned the judgment, acquitting the defendant on the merits because the alleged facts did not occur (perché il fatto non sussiste).

Acquittal on appeal — Conviction overturned
