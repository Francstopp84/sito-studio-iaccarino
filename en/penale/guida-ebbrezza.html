Stopped while driving after drinking?
Your driving licence and your criminal record are at stake.

Driving under the influence is the most frequently prosecuted road traffic offence in Italy. Under the 2024 Highway Code Reform (L. 177/2024), penalties have been further tightened. However, not every assessment is procedurally sound, and not every charge is unassailable. A criminal defence lawyer can make a difference.

Relevant legal provision

What the law provides

Highway Code

Art. 186 — Driving under the influence of alcohol

Driving in a state of intoxication resulting from the consumption of alcoholic beverages is prohibited. A driver found with a blood alcohol concentration exceeding 0.5 g/l is subject to administrative and criminal penalties proportionate to the blood alcohol level.

Penalty: fine up to € 6,000 + arrest up to 1 year

The three severity tiers

Tier 1: 0.5 – 0.8 g/l

Administrative violation. Financial penalty from € 543 to € 2,170. Driving licence suspension from 3 to 6 months. Deduction of 10 points. No criminal relevance.

Tier 2: 0.8 – 1.5 g/l

Criminal offence (misdemeanour / contravvenzione). Fine (ammenda) from € 800 to € 3,200 + detention (arresto) up to 6 months. Driving licence suspension from 6 months to 1 year. Confiscation of the vehicle if the level is > 1.5 or if the vehicle is owned by the offender.

Tier 3: over 1.5 g/l

Serious criminal offence. Fine (ammenda) from € 1,500 to € 6,000 + detention (arresto) from 6 months to 1 year. Driving licence suspension from 1 to 2 years. Confiscation of the vehicle. Revocation of the driving licence in the event of reoffending within two years.

Updates under the 2024 Highway Code Reform (L. 177/2024): Introduced the mandatory installation of an alcolock (a device that prevents the vehicle from starting in the event of a positive blood alcohol reading) for drivers convicted of driving under the influence. Stricter penalties for novice drivers and professional drivers, for whom the threshold is 0.0 g/l.

How we defend you

Defence strategies

Not all testing is procedurally regular. Not all charges are unassailable. Our law firm identifies every flaw in procedure and evidence to build the most effective defence.

Breathalyser defects

Verification of regular type-approval, calibration, and maintenance of the device. The absence of a periodic inspection certificate renders the assessment inadmissible (Cass. Sez. IV, n. 42084/2019).

Procedural irregularities

Challenging the failure to provide notice of the right to be assisted by legal counsel (art. 114 disp. att. c.p.p.), and the requirement for a dual measurement carried out at an interval of at least 5 minutes.

Margin of error

Breathalysers have a recognised margin of error of ±5%. A reading of 0.84 g/l could in fact be 0.79 g/l, with radically different consequences regarding applicable penalties.

Defective reasoning

Challenging the absence of clinical symptoms of intoxication in the official assessment report (verbale di accertamento). If the driver displayed no visible signs of impairment, the instrumental reading alone can be contested.

Alternative procedures

Assessment of the advisability of seeking a plea bargain (art. 444 c.p.p. - patteggiamento), a summary trial (giudizio abbreviato), or probation (art. 168-bis c.p. - messa alla prova) to secure the extinguishment of the offence.

Community service

Request for substitution of the penalty with community service (lavori di pubblica utilità) pursuant to art. 186 comma 9-bis CdS, which allows for the extinction of the offence and the revocation of the vehicle confiscation.

Results achieved

5 brilliantly resolved cases

Case no. 1

Uncalibrated breathalyzer — Tribunale di Napoli

Alleged blood alcohol level: 1,2 g/l — Risk: detention (arresto) + license suspension for 12 months

Professional stopped after a business dinner with a blood alcohol level of 1.2 g/l (tier 2). The defense acquired the maintenance log of the breathalyzer used and demonstrated that the device had not undergone the mandatory annual inspection required by D.M. 196/1990. The Court declared the instrumental assessment unusable.

In the absence of valid proof of the blood alcohol level, the judge acquitted the defendant because the act did not occur, with full return of the driver's license and release of the vehicle from seizure.

Full acquittal — License returned
Case no. 2

Failure to notify the defense attorney — Court of Rome

Contested level: 1.7 g/l — Risk: arrest up to 1 year + vehicle confiscation

Young worker stopped at night with a level of 1.7 g/l (tier 3). The assessment report showed that the officers had not informed the driver of the right to be assisted by a defense attorney of their choice before undergoing the breathalyzer test, in violation of art. 114 disp. att. c.p.p. (implementing provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure).

The defense raised an objection regarding the nullity of the assessment due to a violation of the right to defense. The Court accepted the objection and declared the breathalyzer result unusable, acquitting the defendant.

Acquittal — Assessment declared null
Case no. 3

Decisive margin of error — Court of Milan

Contested level: 0.84 g/l — Risk: transition from administrative offense to criminal offense

Employee stopped at a checkpoint with a blood alcohol level of 0.84 g/l in the first measurement and 0.81 g/l in the second. The level appeared in the criminally relevant range (over 0.8 g/l). The defense produced a technical expert report demonstrating that, by applying the ±5% margin of error recognized by scientific literature and the case law of the Court of Cassation, the actual level could be lower than 0.8 g/l.

The judge reclassified the act as an administrative offense rather than a criminal offense, avoiding a criminal conviction and a compromised criminal record.

Reclassification to administrative offense — No criminal record
Case no. 4

Probation with extinction of the offense — Court of Naples North

Contested level: 1.4 g/l — Risk: arrest + 12-month license suspension + criminal record

Merchant with no prior criminal record, stopped with a level of 1.4 g/l. The assessment was formally regular, making a defense on technical-procedural grounds difficult. The defense strategy focused on the request for probation (messa alla prova) pursuant to art. 168-bis c.p. (Criminal Code), demonstrating the client's clean record, their social and professional roots, and their willingness to perform community service.

The Court granted probation with a program of 180 hours of community service. At the end of the period, the offense was declared extinguished with deletion from the criminal record.

Offense extinguished — No criminal record
Case no. 5

Community service — Court of Salerno

Contested level: 1.9 g/l — Risk: 1-year arrest + vehicle confiscation + license revocation

Artisan stopped after a social event with an extremely high level of 1.9 g/l (maximum tier 3). Assessment was regular, no procedural defects detectable. The vehicle was essential for professional activity. The defense negotiated the substitution of the custodial sentence with community service pursuant to art. 186 paragraph 9-bis CdS (Highway Code), obtaining the revocation of the vehicle confiscation and the halving of the license suspension period.

Upon completion of the work, the offense was declared extinguished. The client was able to resume their professional activity without significant interruptions.

Sentence substituted — Offense extinguished — Vehicle returned
