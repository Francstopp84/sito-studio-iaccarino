Your problem is real

When civil law enters your life

A breached contract, an accident that changed your daily life, a disputed inheritance, an injunction for payment ordering you to pay an amount you do not believe is due: civil law touches people in their most practical and often most painful moments.

The issue is that in civil litigation, timeframes are lengthy, procedures are complex, and mistakes made in the initial stages can prove costly. A poorly drafted originating pleading, an unmet procedural deadline, or an objection not raised in a timely manner can irreversibly prejudice your right. With the Cartabia Reform, civil procedure underwent profound changes that require up-to-date technical mastery.

Our law firm assists you with a legal defence built on in-depth knowledge of current laws — including the changes introduced by the Cartabia Reform (D.Lgs. 149/2022) — and on the most recent case law of the Court of Cassation (giurisprudenza consolidata). Every pleading is drafted with the technical rigour necessary to withstand any challenge.

Results achieved

Real cases, concrete outcomes

Opposition to an injunction order — Tribunale di Napoli

Injunction revoked

Client served with an injunction order (decreto ingiuntivo) for over € 85,000 based on disputed commercial invoices. The opposition established that the debt was not due on account of the opposing party's breach of the original contractual obligations, securing the complete revocation of the order with an award of legal costs against the opposing party.

Compensation for damages — Medical malpractice

Compensation obtained

Patient victim of a diagnostic error with permanent consequences on earning capacity. The defense coordinated the medical-legal expert reports, established the causal link with solid documentation, and obtained compensation including biological, moral, and loss-of-income damages, significantly higher than the initial settlement offer from the opposing party.

Contested succession — Court of Appeal (Corte d’Appello)

Favourable settlement

Forced heir (erede legittimario) excluded by a holographic will. The reduction action (azione di riduzione), supported by a handwriting analysis and rigorous reconstruction of the estate assets, led the counterparty to accept a settlement that fully recognized the reserved statutory share (quota di legittima), avoiding the delays and costs of appellate proceedings.
