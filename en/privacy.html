2. Personal Data Processed

2.1 Data provided voluntarily

Through the contact forms, the user may provide: first and last name, email, telephone number, nationality (immigration only), information relating to the legal case. Providing this data is optional but necessary in order to process the request.

2.2 Data collected automatically

The website does not use profiling cookies or third-party tracking tools. Only anonymous technical data strictly necessary for its operation are collected.

3. Purposes and Legal Basis

  • Responding to consultation requests (art. 6, par. 1, lett. b) GDPR)
  • Fulfillment of legal obligations related to the practice of the legal profession (art. 6, par. 1, lett. c) GDPR)
  • Legitimate interest in the management of professional activities (art. 6, par. 1, lett. f) GDPR)

Information concerning the legal case is processed with the safeguard of professional legal privilege pursuant to art. 6 of the Italian Legal Code of Conduct (Codice Deontologico Forense).

4. Data Retention

Data are retained for the time necessary to handle the request and to fulfill applicable statutory obligations. In the absence of an established professional relationship, data are deleted within 12 months of receipt of the request.

5. Communication of Data

Data are not transferred, sold, or communicated to third parties, except for:

  • Formspree Inc.: form transmission service provider (data processor ex art. 28 GDPR). Policy at formspree.io
  • Judicial or administrative authorities, where required by law
  • Professionals involved in handling the case, strictly within the necessary limits

6. Rights of the Data Subject

Pursuant to artt. 15–22 GDPR, the data subject has the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection to processing. The data subject also has the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority (Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali).

To exercise these rights: studiolegalefrancescoiaccarino@outlook.com. The Data Controller will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

The website uses only technical cookies necessary for its operation, which do not require consent. No profiling or advertising tracking cookies are used.

  • Session cookies: temporary, deleted upon closing the browser
  • Local preferences: store the user's choice on the cookie banner (localStorage), without sending data to external servers

8. Security

The website uses the HTTPS protocol for the secure transmission of data. The forms include anti-spam protection systems. Transmitted data are encrypted during transmission.

9. Amendments to the Privacy Policy

The Data Controller reserves the right to update this privacy policy to adapt it to any regulatory changes. Any amendments will be published on this page indicating the date of update.
