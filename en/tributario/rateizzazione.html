Art. 19 D.P.R. 602/1973

Do you have tax debts that you cannot pay in a single installment?
An installment plan stops attachments, administrative seizures, and mortgages.

An installment plan for tax debt is the instrument that allows taxpayers experiencing financial difficulty to defer payment of their enrolled tax debts into up to 120 monthly installments, while suspending any enforcement action currently underway (attachment [pignoramento], administrative seizure [fermo amministrativo], mortgage lien [ipoteca]) and returning the taxpayer to compliant standing with the Tax Authorities. A well-structured installment plan can mean the difference between restoring financial stability and financial collapse. Our firm assists taxpayers in submitting the application, managing the repayment plan and, in cases of forfeiture, obtaining readmission to the installment plan through the procedures introduced by D.Lgs. 110/2024.

Regulatory framework

The regulation of tax debt installment plans

Art. 19 D.P.R. 29 September 1973, no. 602

Ordinary and extraordinary installment plans: requirements

Article 19 of D.P.R. 602/1973, substantially revised by D.Lgs. 110/2024, is the cornerstone provision governing the deferred payment of debts enrolled in the tax roll and entrusted to the Collection Agent (Agente della Riscossione). The ordinary installment plan is granted upon application by the taxpayer and allows debt payment in a variable number of monthly installments up to a maximum of 72 (ninety-eight for debts exceeding € 120,000), without the need to document the reasons for financial difficulty when the debt does not exceed € 120,000. For debts exceeding € 120,000, the taxpayer must attach evidence demonstrating a temporary situation of objective difficulty. The extraordinary installment plan, on the other hand, can extend up to 120 monthly installments and is reserved for taxpayers in a state of serious and proven financial hardship, documented by producing the liquidity ratio and alpha ratio (for individuals) or the ISEE equivalent economic situation indicator (for individuals) or specific financial ratios (for companies and professionals). The granting of the installment plan triggers the suspension of all enforcement proceedings already underway, with the exception of procedures that were definitively concluded prior to the submission of the application.

Art. 19 D.P.R. 602/1973 — D.Lgs. 110/2024
Forfeiture: art. 19, paragraph 3, D.P.R. 602/1973

Forfeiture of the installment plan: eight unpaid installments

The taxpayer forfeits the benefit of the installment plan when failing to pay eight installments, even if not consecutive. Prior to the reform enacted by D.Lgs. 110/2024, forfeiture was triggered upon non-payment of five non-consecutive installments; the reform raised the threshold to eight installments, making the repayment plan more resilient to temporary financial hardships. Forfeiture entails serious consequences: the remaining debt becomes immediately due and payable in full, all limitation periods for enforcement actions resume, and the taxpayer is registered as a defaulting debtor. Forfeiture does not preclude the possibility of requesting a new installment plan, but this is subject to more stringent conditions and, in the event of repeated forfeiture, may be denied by the Collection Agent. D.Lgs. 110/2024 also introduced the possibility of automatic readmission to the installment plan for taxpayers who forfeited between 2022 and 2024.

Art. 19, para. 3, D.P.R. 602/1973 — 8 installments
D.Lgs. 29 July 2024, no. 110 — Collection Reform

2024 reforms: readmission and new thresholds

D.Lgs. 110/2024, implementing the tax reform enabling act Law 111/2023, introduced significant changes to the rules on installment plans starting from 1 January 2025. The main changes include: (i) the progressive increase of the maximum number of ordinary installments from 72 to 120 (fully operational from 2027); (ii) raising the forfeiture threshold from 5 to 8 installments; (iii) the introduction of automatic readmission to the installment plan for taxpayers who defaulted between 2022 and 2024, subject to filing a specific application by 31 July 2025; (iv) simplification of the documentation required for extraordinary installment plans; (v) introduction of multi-year installment plans for business crises. The decree also modified the calculation of late payment interest applied to installments, making it more favorable for taxpayers than in the past. Our firm constantly monitors regulatory developments to provide clients with the most up-to-date and advantageous installment solutions.

D.Lgs. 110/2024 — Collection reform

Payment in installments (rateizzazione) suspends attachments (pignoramenti), administrative vehicle holds (fermi), and mortgages (ipoteche). From the moment the installment application (domanda di rateizzazione) is submitted until the decision granting (or denying) it is issued, the Collection Agent (Agente della Riscossione) cannot initiate new enforcement actions. Once payment in installments is granted, all enforcement proceedings already underway are suspended for the entire duration of the plan. The suspension of administrative holds (fermi amministrativi) allows driving the vehicle subject to the hold again; the suspension of the mortgage (ipoteca) allows resuming negotiations for the sale or financing of the property. Submitting the application correctly is the crucial first step: an incomplete or late application (istanza) may jeopardize these protective effects.

The factual circumstances

Types of installment plans

The choice of the most appropriate installment plan depends on the amount of the debt, the taxpayer's economic situation, and whether enforcement proceedings are currently pending. Our firm assesses the optimal solution on a case-by-case basis.

Standard

Ordinary installment plan (72 installments)

For debts up to € 120.000, ordinary installment payment (rateizzazione ordinaria) is obtained through a simple online application to the collection agent (Agente della Riscossione), without the need to produce proof of income. The amortization schedule (piano di ammortamento) is based on fixed installments with interest calculated at the statutory rate. Submitting the application immediately suspends any enforcement action (azione esecutiva), and the taxpayer is considered “in compliance” (in regola) with the tax authorities (Fisco) for all legal purposes.

Reference: Art. 19, c. 1, D.P.R. 602/1973
Severe hardship

Extraordinary installment plan (120 installments)

For taxpayers who demonstrate that they are facing severe and proven financial hardship, it is possible to obtain up to 120 monthly installments. For natural persons, the documentation requires the submission of the ISEE (Equivalent Economic Situation Indicator); for businesses and professionals, the liquidity and alpha ratios derived from accounting records are required. The extraordinary plan significantly reduces the amount of each individual installment, making the debt manageable even in severe crisis situations.

Reference: Art. 19, c. 1-bis, D.P.R. 602/1973
Readmission

Readmission after forfeiture (decadenza)

A taxpayer who has forfeited a previous installment plan may request a new extension. Pursuant to D.Lgs. 110/2024, readmission is automatic for those who forfeited between 2022 and 2024 who submit an application by 31 July 2025. For earlier forfeitures, it is nonetheless possible to submit a new application by demonstrating an improvement in one's financial circumstances compared to the time of forfeiture. Our firm assists taxpayers in preparing the documentation required for readmission.

Reference: D.Lgs. 110/2024
Natural person

Installment plans for private individuals

For natural persons, the application for an ordinary installment plan (rateizzazione ordinaria) (up to € 120.000) does not require income documentation and is submitted online or at the counter of the Collection Agent (Agente della Riscossione). For an extraordinary installment plan (rateizzazione straordinaria), it is necessary to provide a currently valid ISEE certificate (modello ISEE) and a declaration of asset status (dichiarazione della situazione patrimoniale). The number of installments is determined based on the household’s ISEE, in accordance with tables predetermined by law.

Reference: Art. 19 D.P.R. 602/1973
Company

Installment plans for companies and businesses

For companies and sole proprietorships, an extraordinary installment plan requires the production of updated accounting records (financial statements, interim accounting statements, bank statements) and the calculation of liquidity and alpha ratios. Demonstrating the temporary nature of the economic difficulty and the prospects for recovery is essential to obtain a long-term plan. Our firm liaises with accounting professionals for the optimal preparation of the documentation.

Reference: Art. 19, c. 1-bis, D.P.R. 602/1973
Extension

Extension of the current plan

A taxpayer who already has an ongoing instalment plan (piano di rateizzazione) and is experiencing difficulty paying the instalments may request an extension of the plan, with a reduction in the amount of the individual instalments and an extension of the total number of months. The extension is granted when the taxpayer’s financial circumstances have deteriorated significantly compared to the time the plan was originally granted. It is necessary to submit a specific application to the Collection Agent (Agente della Riscossione) before forfeiture (decadenza) occurs due to failure to pay the instalments.

Reference: Art. 19, c. 4, D.P.R. 602/1973

Our method

The six assistance strategies

The management of a tax instalment plan (rateizzazione fiscale) does not end with the submission of the application: it requires constant monitoring, managing any difficulties in complying with the plan, and a prompt response in the event of forfeiture (decadenza) or challenges raised by the tax collection agent (Agente della Riscossione).

I.

Debt analysis and optimal strategy

Before submitting the application, we analyze the taxpayer’s overall debt to verify whether there are time-barred claims (crediti prescritti), unlawful tax assessment notices (avvisi di accertamento), or calculation errors that allow reducing the debt base to be paid in installments. A lower debt means lower installments and a more sustainable plan. The analysis also includes assessing the advisability of challenging part of the debt in parallel with the installment plan (rateizzazione).

II.

Preparation of the application

Comprehensive assistance in preparing and submitting the installment application, including gathering and properly presenting income and asset documentation. For extraordinary installment plans, calculation of the required ratios (liquidity, alpha, ISEE) and preparation of the explanatory report detailing the taxpayer's financial hardship, which is decisive for obtaining the maximum number of installments.

III.

Urgent stay of enforcement proceedings

In cases where an attachment (pignoramento), an administrative seizure (fermo amministrativo), or a mortgage (ipoteca) is already underway and the client needs to urgently block the enforcement action, we submit the application for payment by installments (rateizzazione) using an urgent procedure. The submission of the application, including via electronic means, results in the immediate suspension of pending enforcement actions, allowing the taxpayer to immediately regain the availability of the seized vehicle or to unblock the mortgaged property.

IV.

Monitoring and management of the plan

Ongoing assistance throughout the entire installment plan (rateizzazione) period: monitoring installment due dates, verifying the correct calculation of interest, managing communications with the Collection Agent (Agente della Riscossione), and promptly reporting any errors in the repayment schedule (piano di ammortamento). In the event of temporary difficulties in paying the installments, assistance in requesting an extension of the plan before forfeiture (decadenza) occurs.

V.

Readmission after forfeiture (decadenza)

In cases where the taxpayer has already forfeited the installment plan, assistance in the readmission procedure: verifying eligibility for automatic readmission under D.Lgs. 110/2024, preparing the readmission petition, gathering the documentation required to demonstrate an improved financial standing, and submitting the new installment application within statutory deadlines, thereby preventing the resumption of enforcement proceedings.

VI.

Integration with a tax appeal (ricorso tributario)

In many cases, the optimal strategy combines payment in instalments (rateizzazione) of uncontestable amounts with a tax appeal (ricorso tributario) against sums that appear unlawful. This dual strategy makes it possible to: suspend enforcement actions on the entire debt (thanks to the instalment plan), reduce the amount to be paid in instalments through the judicial annulment of the unlawful portion, and maximise overall savings for the taxpayer.

Case No. 1 — Extraordinary installment plan (rateizzazione straordinaria)

Entrepreneur in financial distress: 120-installment plan at € 1.200/month for a debt of € 138.000

Revenue Agency–Collection (Agenzia delle Entrate–Riscossione) / Naples — Year 2024

A restaurant entrepreneur came to the firm with an overall debt of € 138,000 to the Agenzia delle Entrate–Riscossione, consisting of unpaid VAT, withholding taxes, and INPS contributions for the years 2020–2022, accrued during the industry crisis linked to the pandemic. The Tax Collection Agent (Agente della Riscossione) had already served preliminary acts for the expropriation of company assets. The client had a recovering business but was unable to sustain a monthly installment exceeding € 1,500. Our firm prepared the application for an extraordinary installment plan (rateizzazione straordinaria), accompanied by accounting records for the last two financial years, the interim accounting statement updated to the preceding quarter, and the calculation of the liquidity and alpha ratios demonstrating the temporary nature of the financial difficulty and the turnaround prospect. The Tax Collection Agent (Agente della Riscossione) granted the extraordinary plan of 120 monthly installments, with an installment of € 1,380 inclusive of principal and interest, enabling the entrepreneur to continue operations without further enforcement pressures. All attachments (pignoramenti) already served were simultaneously stayed.

120 installments — € 1.380/month — Enforcement proceedings stayed
Case no. 2 — Readmission pursuant to D.Lgs. 110/2024

Defaulted in 2023: readmission to installment plan (rateizzazione) in 45 days

Revenue Agency–Collection (Agenzia delle Entrate–Riscossione) / Naples — Year 2025

A woman running a clothing store had obtained an ordinary installment plan (rateizzazione ordinaria) in 2021 for an overall debt of € 72,000, payable in 72 monthly installments. Due to financial difficulties of the business in 2022 and 2023, she had missed nine consecutive installments between August 2022 and April 2023, resulting in forfeiture (decadenza) of the installment plan. From that point, the Tax Collection Agent (Agente della Riscossione) resumed enforcement actions, serving a bank account attachment (pignoramento del conto corrente) on the business account that effectively blocked the store’s operations. Our firm identified the client as falling within the scope of application of the automatic readmission provided for by art. 19-ter D.P.R. 602/1973, introduced by D.Lgs. 110/2024, for taxpayers who defaulted between 2022 and 2024. The firm prepared and submitted the readmission application with all necessary documentation within statutory deadlines. The Tax Collection Agent (Agente della Riscossione) granted readmission within 45 days of filing the application, with a new plan of 120 monthly installments and the simultaneous release of the bank account from attachment. The store was able to resume its commercial activity.

Readmission in 45 days — Bank account unblocked
Case No. 3 — Installment plan (rateizzazione) + Appeal (ricorso)

Combined strategy: installment plan for € 82.000 + appeal on € 45.000

Revenue Agency - Collection (Agenzia delle Entrate-Riscossione) / CGT I grado Napoli — Year 2024

A construction company received from the Agenzia delle Entrate–Riscossione a tax roll burden (iscritti a ruolo) for € 127,000, consisting of: an IRPEF tax assessment (accertamento) for € 82,000 relating to the director (years 2018–2019) and alleged VAT omissions for € 45,000 (year 2020) on transactions that had been regularly recorded in the accounting ledgers and for which the tax had already been paid. Our firm’s analysis identified two distinct debt categories: one legitimate but unsustainable as a lump sum, the other tainted by an obvious error on the part of the Tax Authority. The strategy involved: (i) immediate submission of an application for an extraordinary installment plan (rateizzazione straordinaria) for the IRPEF debt of € 82,000, obtaining 120 monthly installments of € 820, with simultaneous suspension of all pending enforcement actions; (ii) filing an appeal (ricorso) before the CGT di I grado for the VAT debt of € 45,000, enclosing all accounting documentation proving payment. The VAT appeal (ricorso) was granted in full, with the annulment of the tax assessment and an order for legal costs. The installment plan on the legitimate remaining balance is proceeding regularly.

Installment plan (rateizzazione) + Annulment € 45,000
Case No. 4 — Administrative seizure of a vehicle (fermo amministrativo)

Vehicle impoundment lifted within 48 hours thanks to urgent instalment payment plan

Revenue Agency–Collection (Agenzia delle Entrate–Riscossione) / Naples — Year 2024

A commercial sales representative came to our law firm in a state of panic: the day before, he had discovered that his company car had been placed under an administrative seizure (fermo amministrativo) for a personal income tax (IRPEF) debt of € 38,500 relating to the years 2019 and 2020. Without the vehicle, he was unable to carry out his work and faced the risk of losing his clients. The urgency was absolute. Our firm prepared and submitted electronically, within the very same day, an application for a standard instalment payment plan (rateizzazione ordinaria) for € 38,500 requesting 72 monthly instalments. At the same time, the firm notified the tax collection agent (Agente della Riscossione) of the submission of the application, requesting the concurrent suspension of the administrative seizure pursuant to art. 19, paragraph 6, D.P.R. 602/1973, which specifically provides for the suspension of enforcement actions from the date of submission of the application. Within 48 hours of filing the application, the client was able to go to the offices of the collection agent (Agente della Riscossione) to obtain the order suspending the administrative seizure and secure the immediate release of the vehicle. The standard instalment plan was subsequently formalized over the following days with 72 monthly instalments of approximately € 570 each. The client was able to resume his business activities normally.

Vehicle seizure lifted in 48 hours
Case No. 5 — Extension of instalment plan

Extension of an ongoing instalment plan: instalments reduced from € 1,800 to € 950 for a pensioner

Revenue Agency – Collection (*Agenzia delle Entrate–Riscossione*) / Naples — Year 2023

In 2020, a 71-year-old pensioner had obtained an extraordinary instalment plan (rateizzazione straordinaria) of 120 instalments for an IRPEF debt of € 95,000, with a monthly instalment of € 1,800 inclusive of interest. The plan had been structured taking into account the employment income the taxpayer was earning at the time. Following early retirement, his monthly income decreased from € 4,200 to € 2,100, making the monthly instalment unsustainable (it represented 86% of his disposable income). The client contacted our firm fearing forfeiture of the plan (decadenza) due to the impossibility of paying future instalments. Our firm submitted an application for the extension of the ongoing plan, documenting the significant change in the taxpayer's income situation through the production of his pension slip, the notice from the national social security institute (INPS) regarding the pension amount, and the newly updated equivalent economic situation indicator (ISEE). The collection agent (Agente della Riscossione) granted the extension of the plan with a reduction of the monthly instalment to € 950 and a corresponding extension of the remaining repayment period. The client was able to continue honoring the plan without risk of forfeiture and with a sustainable impact on his household budget.

Instalment reduced from € 1,800 to € 950/month
