About Me

Avv. Francesco Iaccarino

Palazzo di Domenico Barbaja, Naples

Enrolled in the Bar Association of Naples (Albo degli Avvocati del Foro di Napoli), I practice law from my chambers located in the prestigious Palazzo di Domenico Barbaja, in the heart of the city. My background is rooted in a family tradition of legal professionals — my grandfather a lawyer, my grandmother a civil-law notary — and has been consolidated through years of practice before the Courts and Courts of Appeal of Italy.

My philosophy is straightforward: every case warrants an in-depth examination, a defence strategy tailored to the specific facts, and comprehensive mastery of the most recent case law. I do not believe in standardized defences. I believe in technical rigour, meticulous preparation, and the determination of one who handles every mandate as if it were the only one.

I operate throughout the national territory — from Piedmont to Sicily — providing assistance at every level of jurisdiction: from the initial hearing to appeals before the Court of Cassation (impugnazione in grado di appello), with direct appearance in the courtrooms of every Italian Court.

Why Choose Us

What distinguishes our practice

01

Tailored strategy

Each case is examined according to its specific facts. We do not use template defences: the strategy stems from a thorough study of the facts, applicable rules, and the most recent case law.

02

Continuous professional updating

Law changes daily. We actively monitor rulings from the Court of Cassation (giurisprudenza consolidata), legislative reforms, and case law trends to provide an up-to-date and legally grounded defence.

03

Complete transparency

From the first consultation, you will receive an assessment of the case, the realistic prospects of success, and the legal fees. No surprises, no unfounded promises.

04

Nationwide coverage

From our chambers in Naples, we handle matters throughout Italy. Whether your proceedings are in Milan, Rome, Palermo, or any other city, we offer the same quality of legal assistance and courtroom attendance.

05

Prompt response

We understand that in legal matters, time is of the essence. We commit to contacting you within a few hours of your inquiry with an initial evaluation of your matter.

06

Direct relationship

You will speak directly with the lawyer handling your case, not with intermediaries or secretarial staff. The relationship of trust between lawyer and client is the foundation of any effective defence.

Information on legal consultation

The first legal consultation, in the office in Naples (Via Toledo 205) or remotely by phone or video call, has a fixed cost of 200 euros and is entirely deductible from the fee in case of granting the mandate.
