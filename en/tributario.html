The Hidden Danger

Why you must not ignore a tax notice

When a tax collection bill (cartella esattoriale), a notice of tax assessment (avviso di accertamento), or an order to pay (intimazione di pagamento) from the Revenue-Collection Agency (Agenzia delle Entrate-Riscossione) arrives, the initial reaction is often panic or, conversely, underestimation. Both are hazardous. Panic leads to paying sums that may not even be due. Underestimation leads to missing the statutory deadlines to lodge an appeal, rendering the act final, enforceable, and no longer subject to challenge.

The truth is that a significant percentage of tax collection notices issued by the tax administration suffer from defects that render them voidable: improper service, expiration of the assessment periods, time-barred debts (prescrizione), calculation errors, or lack of statement of reasons. However, these defects must be identified promptly by a legal professional and contested before the appropriate judicial bodies within strict, non-extendable statutory time limits.

Our firm is dedicated to the analysis of tax acts and the identification of defects that permit their full or partial annulment. We work with the most up-to-date case law of the Courts of Tax Justice (Giudici Tributari) and the Court of Cassation (giurisprudenza consolidata), fully applying the innovations of the Tax Justice Reform (L. 130/2022) and the Tax Reform (L. 111/2023).

Results achieved

Real cases, concrete outcomes

Time-barred tax bills (cartelle esattoriali) — CGT I grado

Total annulment

Company recipient of tax bills (cartelle esattoriali) for a total of € 120,000 relating to taxes dating back more than seven years. The appeal (ricorso) demonstrated that the five-year statute of limitations on the tax claims had expired, in accordance with the established case law of the Court of Cassation. Full annulment of the tax bills with the tax authority ordered to pay legal costs.

VAT assessment notice (avviso di accertamento) — CGT II grado

70% reduction

Entrepreneur served with a notice of assessment (avviso di accertamento) for alleged VAT evasion amounting to € 95,000. The defence challenged the inductive methodology used by the Agency, demonstrating the inconsistency of the presumptions applied and producing exculpatory banking and accounting documentation. In the second instance, a reduction of over 70% of the tax claim was obtained.

Seizure revoked

Artisan whose work vehicle was subjected to an administrative seizure (fermo amministrativo) for allegedly overdue tax debts. The urgent petition for precautionary relief obtained the suspension of collection and the revocation of the seizure, demonstrating both the irreparable harm to the business activity and the defects in the underlying act, which was never validly served.

Our method

How we handle your tax case

1

Analysis of the act

We examine the tax assessment act line by line: validity of service, compliance with statutory forfeiture (decadenza) and limitation (prescrizione) periods, accuracy of calculation, and adequacy of the statement of reasons. Nothing is taken for granted.

2

Identification of defects

We identify every aspect of unlawfulness — both formal and substantive — that allows for the total or partial annulment of the act, in light of the most recent tax case law.

3

Choice of strategy

We evaluate whether to proceed through administrative review (autotutela), mandatory complaint-mediation (reclamo-mediazione), judicial appeal (ricorso giurisdizionale), or a combination of these avenues, depending on the specific nature of the case and the amount of the claim.

4

Defence and urgent suspension

If necessary, we immediately request the suspension of collection to prevent irreparable harm. We then proceed with the defence on the merits, up to the final available level of jurisdiction.

Deadlines expire

You have 60 days to appeal. Do not wait until the last minute.

The deadlines to challenge a tax bill (cartella esattoriale) or a notice of assessment (avviso di accertamento) are strict: generally 60 days from service. Afterward, the act becomes final and can no longer be challenged in any forum. Every day that passes is one day less.
